What's on today, what's coming up, and what's already done.

Daily check-ins

Weigh in and log your food and steps each day, and see any tasks your coach has set you, like progress photos. Each check-in ticks itself off when you do it.

Where to find itToday → Still to do

Your schedule

Every workout and task, by the week or the month, with what's done and what's still to come.

Move a workout

Where to find itOpen a workout → ⋯ → Move to another day

Workouts

Your plan, your sets, and everything that helps you through a session.

Your plans

Every plan your coach has given you in one place, each day with its exercises and when you last did it.

Where to find itWorkouts

My workouts

Build your own workout from the exercise library, save it, and start it or put it on your schedule whenever you like.

Where to find itWorkouts → My workouts → New

A workout day

Open any day to see each exercise, the muscles it works and what you did last time, then start it whenever suits you.

Where to find itWorkouts → a day

Log as you go

Tick off each set as you finish it. Last time's numbers sit beside every set, whichever workout or plan they came from, and one tap fills them in. Typed the wrong number? Change it on a ticked set and it unticks itself, ready to tick again. Supersets are grouped, so you move round by round and rest at the end of each round.

Where to find itToday → Start workout

Focus mode and rest timer

One exercise and one set at a time, in big type. A rest countdown starts after each set, and you can add or take off 15 seconds. It shows on your Lock Screen too, so you can put your phone down.

Where to find itIn a workout → Focus mode

Demo videos

Not sure of the form? Watch a short demo of any exercise, along with the muscles it works.

Where to find itIn a workout → Watch

Swap an exercise

Equipment taken? Swap to a similar exercise for today, with the closest matches first. Your target stays the same.

Where to find itIn a workout → the swap button beside an exercise

Runs and activities

Runs track your distance and time. Activities like a badminton session are a simple tick once you've done them.

Where to find itA run day in your plan

Stretching sessions

Follow your coach's stretches, with their tips for each one, then tap Finish. Nothing to type in.

Where to find itWorkouts → a stretching day

Go for a best

No record this session? Before you finish, you'll get one set that could set a new best. Go for it, or skip.

Where to find itTap Finish at the end of a workout

Workout recap

Your time, total weight lifted and sets done, how it compares with last time, and any new bests, ready to share.

Where to find itAfter you finish, or ⋯ → Recap on a finished workout

Feedback from your coach

Your coach can leave a note on a finished workout, right where you'll see it.

Where to find itOpen a finished workout

Friends

Train together

Invite up to three friends to your workout. Each of you logs your own sets, and you can see how everyone's doing as you go.
